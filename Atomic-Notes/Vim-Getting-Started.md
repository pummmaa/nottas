# Table Of Contents

## Vim Modes
To enter these mode press the letter the mode is represented by
1. Normal (n)
2. Insert (i)
3. Visual (v)
4. Command (:)

## Advancy Commands (non modifier) (motion)
- *w* to move *one word* at the time to the right (forward)
- *b* to move *one word* at the time to the left (back)
- *f* press the letter you want to move located on the current line the cursor is on
- *o* to enter into (insert) mode onto the following line

## Advancy Lines Movement (motion)
- press *line number* up *k* or down *j* to move a specific number of lines

## Advancy Commands (using modifier "SHIFT") (motion)
- *Shift+A* to move at the end of the line
- *Shift+I* to move at the beggining of the line
- *Shift+F* press the letter you want to move located on the current line the cursor is on (backwards)
- *Shift+W* to move to white/emphy spaces
- *Shift+B* to move to white/emphy spaces (backwards)
- *Shift+O* creates a new line above and enters (insert) mode
